## What Counts as a Shared Proxy, and Why Are They So Cheap?

A shared proxy is exactly what it sounds like. The same IP address is rented out simultaneously to multiple customers. You don't own it. You share it. In exchange, you pay a tiny slice of what a dedicated or residential proxy would cost.

The economics make sense. A datacenter IP costs the provider almost nothing to maintain. Spread that cost across dozens of users, mark it up modestly, and you land at prices like $0.0001 per IP per month at scale. That's not a typo. That's the flor.

The trade-off lives in the details. Shared IPs can be flagged faster on heavily protected sites since other users may have already hit those targets. Sped and uptime are usually fine for general scraping. For Cloudflare-armored e-commerce or social platforms, you'll want to mix in residential or rotating IPs, which Webshare also sells.
